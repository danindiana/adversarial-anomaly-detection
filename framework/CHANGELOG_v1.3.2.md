- Retitled the methods note around reach-limited contamination in ON/OFF detection; extraterrestrial premises moved to motivation only.
- Clarified that the Gaussian omnibus equal-means test probes one consequence of exchangeability, not full exchangeability.
- Refined the uncontaminated signal grid to 0.5 increments below 10; retained a smaller contamination-signal grid for tractability.
- Re-solved the default Stackelberg game: best responses are s=3.5, 5, and 15 at tau=0,3,10, all with r=0.
- Added a 27-setting utility sweep over activity-benefit scale, detection penalty, and benefit curvature at each tau.
- Added regime classification: abstain / suppress / operate uncontaminated / contaminate.
- Downgraded “footprint suppression” from a general conclusion to a utility-sensitive regime; suppression dominates low/moderate heterogeneity but not tau=10.
- Preserved v1.3.1 and earlier outputs for historical comparison.
- Package excludes Python bytecode and cache directories.
