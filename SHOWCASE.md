# Showcase framing

## Working title

**How Do You Detect Something That Knows You're Looking?**

Talk title at the AI InTheRock Local AI Showcase (November 18, 2026): **Hiding in the Noise**.

## One-sentence pitch

A reproducible AI-assisted research project on adversarial anomaly detection: what happens when the hidden actor can change its footprint, exploit correlated background noise, and interfere with the observer's controls?

## Live-demo concept

Use a real or synthetic multichannel dataset to show three stages:

1. a naive single-channel anomaly detector;
2. a covariance-aware multichannel detector;
3. an adaptive contamination attack that exploits a weakly calibrated direction.

Then randomize the observer's test or improve covariance calibration and show how the actor's best response changes.

A shorter alternative: a screen recording of one review round, where one AI's package is handed to the other, which re-runs it from a clean directory and finds a discrepancy.

## Ask

We are looking for:

- a serious real-world multichannel dataset;
- datasets with a known hidden actor (seeded fraud, red-team logs, sensor spoofing);
- domain experts willing to attack the model;
- collaborators in statistics, signal processing, cybersecurity, sensor systems, or robust ML;
- opportunities to test whether disciplined AI-assisted research can produce useful results with modest compute and no conventional lab infrastructure.
